How it counts

  • One rep = the hands swapping which one is higher, like a see-saw.
  • Nothing is measured against a fixed height, so there is nothing to calibrate and moving up or down in frame does not matter.
  • Pumping the same hand twice cannot count: a swap needs both hands.
  • The shaded band in the meter is the deadband — hands roughly level hold the current side, so jitter never counts.
  • Keep your shoulders and both hands in frame; counting waits until they are visible. Hips are not needed, so you can stand close.
